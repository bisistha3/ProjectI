Why It's Different

Goals made for you, not everyone

calculate

Water goal from your BMI

We use your weight, height, gender, and activity level to calculate a hydration target that fits your body — not a generic "8 glasses" rule.

science

Nutrition from Mifflin-St Jeor

Your calorie and macro targets come from the same formula nutritionists use. It adapts as your weight or activity changes.

fitness_center

Exercise burn from your weight

Calories burned = MET × your weight × time. A 60kg person burns less than a 90kg person doing the same workout. We account for that.

tune

Custom goals anytime

Prefer your own targets? Switch to custom mode and set exact numbers for water, calories, macros, and exercise minutes.
